Tile Trail

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 August 2026 · Tile Trail, by Isanka Diddeniya, Christchurch, New Zealand

The short version: Tile Trail has no accounts and never asks who you are. The game plays fully offline. If you allow it, we receive anonymous gameplay statistics to improve the game — you can turn this off any time in Settings. Ads, where shown, run under the consent rules of your region. This page loads no trackers.

Information stored only on your device

Your progress — coins, levels completed, difficulty state, daily streak, and settings — is saved on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Anonymous gameplay analytics (optional)

With "Share anonymous usage data" switched on in Settings, the game sends gameplay statistics to Google Firebase Analytics: which levels are played, wins and fails, booster use, difficulty values, and session information. These are numbers and categories only — no names, no messages, no personal details — associated with a random app-instance identifier, not with you. Switch the setting off and nothing is sent, from that moment.

Advertising

Tile Trail shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Where your region requires it (for example under the GDPR), a consent dialog appears before any ad; on iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt governs ad tracking. Ad providers may process your device's advertising identifier under those consents. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings, and ad-free play is available through the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details.

What we never collect

No name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or files. The app requests no such permissions.

Sharing

Data described above is processed only by the named providers (Google Firebase, Google AdMob) to operate analytics and advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Analytics data is retained under limited retention periods configured in Firebase, then deleted or aggregated. On-device data is yours and remains until you delete the app.

Children

Tile Trail is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and the GDPR — you may have rights to access, correct, or delete information. Because we hold no account data about you, most requests are satisfied by the Settings opt-out and your device's controls; for anything else, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
